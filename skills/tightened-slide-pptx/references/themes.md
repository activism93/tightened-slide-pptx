# Theme Presets

Use exactly one accent palette per deck.

## International Klein Blue

- Paper: `#FAFAF8`
- Ink: `#0A0A0A`
- Grey 1: `#F0F0EE`
- Grey 2: `#D4D4D2`
- Grey 3: `#737373`
- Accent: `#1B36A6`
- Accent soft: `#E7EBF8`
- Accent light: `#8EA5FF`

Use for analytical, technical, AI, product, and general corporate decks.
